Meet your practitioner

Support that starts with listening.

Monika offers one-to-one sessions shaped around each person’s goals, needs and preferences. Her approach begins with listening, making space for reflection and discussing what kind of support may be appropriate.

The Clarity Space with Monika brings together NLP coaching, hypnosis, life coaching and Reiki. Rather than following one fixed method for everyone, the intention is to understand each person's goals and preferences and discuss an approach that feels appropriate.
