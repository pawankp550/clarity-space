A few things to know

Your questions are welcome.

Do I need to choose an approach before contacting you?

No. You can share what you're looking for and select “not sure yet.” We'll discuss options together.

Can approaches be combined?

You can express interest in any combination. We'll discuss what each approach involves and agree on what is appropriate before proceeding.

Are sessions a replacement for healthcare?

No. Coaching, hypnosis and Reiki services offered here are not a substitute for medical or mental-health diagnosis or treatment.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry with a little information about your goals and preferred format. The practitioner can then follow up to discuss next steps.
